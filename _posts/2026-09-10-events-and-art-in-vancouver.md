I keep having to look up where to find out what's going on in Vancouver, art openings, galleries, live shows, meetups. So here is a list, sorted by category, of where to look. This is a snapshot from September 2026, not a live feed, so check each link for what's current.

## General event calendars

Broad, city-wide listings. Good first stop if you just want to see what's on this week.

- [Do604](https://do604.com/events/today) - Vancouver events listing, concerts, festivals, and things to do, filterable by day.
- [Daily Hive - Listed](https://dailyhive.com/vancouver/listed) - local events section, covers concerts, food/drink events, and pop-ups around the city.
- [Vancouver Is Awesome - Events & Entertainment](https://www.vancouverisawesome.com/events-and-entertainment) - local news site's events section.
- [The Georgia Straight - Listings](https://www.straight.com/listings/events) - Vancouver's long-running alt-weekly, listings cover music, arts, theatre, and film.
- [Scout Magazine - The Scout List](https://scoutmagazine.ca/the-scout-list/) - a curated weekly pick of what to do, food/drink and culture leaning.
- [Luma - Vancouver](https://luma.com/vancouver) - general events calendar/ticketing platform, the Vancouver page aggregates local meetups, workshops, and social events.
- [LampPost Social - Events](https://www.lamppostsocial.com/events) - community events discovery site, aggregates festivals, concerts, theatre, and sports across Vancouver and other Canadian cities.

## Recurring art crawls & gallery nights

- **East Side Culture Crawl** ([culturecrawl.ca](https://culturecrawl.ca)) - mid-November, studios and galleries all over East Van open their doors. Described in the r/askvan thread as "the beating heart of Vancouver's art scene."
- **First Saturday** ([firstsaturday.ca](https://www.firstsaturday.ca/)) - same idea as the Culture Crawl, monthly instead of once a year.
- **Instant Coffee mailing list** ([instantcoffee.org](https://instantcoffee.org/)) - artist collective whose email list gets recommended for openings at VIVO Media Arts, Access Gallery, THIS Gallery, grunt gallery, Western Front, and others.
- [Pecha Kucha Vancouver](https://pechakuchavancouver.com/) - recurring rapid-fire presentation nights, a way to meet the independent gallery and design crowd.

## Galleries & artist-run spaces

A partial list, mostly around Mount Pleasant, Chinatown, and the False Creek Flats.
- [Slice of Life](https://www.slicevancouver.ca/) - gallery on Venables St, no upfront cost to show, rotating exhibits, workshops, gift shop of local work.
- [The Playground Studio](https://www.theplaygroundstudio.ca/) - exhibition space in Mount Pleasant (108 E Broadway), rotating group shows.
- [The Beaumont Studios](https://thebeaumontstudios.com/open-studios/) - one of Vancouver's original creative collectives, hosts open studio events where you can meet the resident artists directly.
- [grunt gallery](https://grunt.ca/) - artist-run centre since 1984, exhibitions, residencies, digital projects.
- [Equinox Gallery](https://www.equinoxgallery.com/) - a large Commercial Drive space, mostly Canadian and local artists.
- **Polygon Gallery** ([thepolygon.ca](https://thepolygon.ca/)) - North Vancouver, right off the SeaBus, photography and contemporary art.
- Others mentioned in the thread: [THIS Gallery](https://thisgallery.org/), [Paul Kyle Gallery](https://www.paulkylegallery.com/), [Gallery Jones](https://www.galleryjones.com/), [Trapp Projects](https://www.trappprojects.com/), [Western Front](https://westernfront.ca/), [Fingerprint Gallery](https://fingerprintgallery.ca/), [Gallery 881](https://www.gallery881.com/), [Pendulum Gallery](http://www.pendulumgallery.bc.ca/) (downtown, opposite the Vancouver Art Gallery), [Catriona Jeffries](https://catrionajeffries.com/), [Monte Clark Gallery](https://monteclarkgallery.com/), [Pale Fire](https://palefireprojects.com/), [Unit Pitt](https://www.unitpitt.ca/), [Unit 17](https://www.unit17.org/), [Or Gallery](https://orgallery.org/), [Cica](https://cicavancouver.com/), [One More Life Gallery](https://www.onemorelifegallery.com/), [Parker St. Gallery](https://www.gallerieswest.ca/locations/parker-street-gallery/), [Sum Gallery](https://sumgallery.ca/).
- [Astro Club Artist Facility](https://www.astroarts.ca/) - studio complex and community arts hub, annual block-party festival, mural jams, artist market.

## Live comedy, theatre & music venues

- [Rio Theatre](https://riotheatre.ca/) - 409-seat independent cinema, built 1938, also runs live comedy, music, and events.
- [Comedy After Dark](https://comedyafterdark.ca/) - comedy club, stand-up with a full diner and cocktail bar.
- [Venue Co-op](https://venue.coop/) - non-profit co-operative of artists, organizers, and music-lovers across Vancouver's DIY music and arts scene, runs and books community-owned venue space.
- [The Rec Room - Burnaby Brentwood](https://www.therecroom.com/burnaby-brentwood) - large entertainment venue (games, live music, food) in Brentwood, just outside Vancouver proper.
- [The Cultch](https://thecultch.com/) - East Van presenting company, brings in touring theatre, dance, and music.
- [The Fox Cabaret](https://www.foxcabaret.com/) - periodic live shows, comedy, and events on Main St.
- Also mentioned: [What Lab](https://whatlab.ca/) (indie DIY theatre/dance/music space), [Little Mountain Gallery](https://littlemountaingallery.ca/) (indie comedy/theatre hybrid nights), theatre companies [Rumble](https://rumble.org/) and [Neworld](https://neworldtheatre.com/).

## Community & meetup directories

- [Vancouver Community](https://vancouvercommunity.org/) - directory of 248+ local groups across 40 categories: run clubs, book clubs, trivia nights, creative meetups, volunteering, and more.

## Festivals

- [Vancouver Fringe Festival](https://www.vancouverfringe.com/) - runs in September on Granville Island, unjuried, so it's a mixed bag of shows.
- **Design Vancouver** ([designvancouver.org](https://designvancouver.org/events-26)) - September design-focused event series.
- [VIFF](https://viff.org/) (Vancouver International Film Festival) - runs in the fall, with an [Off-VIFF](https://filmfreeway.com/OFF-VIFF) underground program in October.

## Other event pages

A few more resources that came up in the thread and didn't fit neatly above:

- [Preview Magazine](https://preview-art.com/) - print and web resource listing gallery openings around the city.
- [CreateAStir](https://www.createastir.ca/) - Vancouver arts and culture events listing.
- [Griffin Art Projects](https://www.griffinartprojects.ca/) (North Van) and the [West Vancouver Art Museum](https://westvancouverartmuseum.ca/) - both run regular contemporary art programming on the North Shore.
- [The Belkin](https://belkin.ubc.ca/) (UBC), [Libby Leshgold Gallery](https://libby.ecuad.ca/) (Emily Carr University), and the [Museum of Anthropology](https://moa.ubc.ca/) (UBC) - campus galleries worth checking for openings.
- [The Gibson](https://gibson.sfu.ca/) (SFU) - runs events and talks, with a new exhibition opening most terms.
- [The Vodville](https://www.atlasobscura.com/places/east-van-vodville-cinema-vancouver) (Woodland & Venables) - a miniature movie theatre that posts local indie event flyers, gets a lot of foot traffic from the arts-curious crowd in the area.
- [Stop Podcasting Yourself](https://maximumfun.org/podcasts/stop-podcasting-yourself/) and [The Town Show](https://thetownshow.com/) - podcasts that cover Vancouver's local indie comedy scene.
